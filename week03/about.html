White Water Rafting Co.

Our mission is to provide unforgettable white water rafting experiences while respecting nature. We believe in adventure, safety, and creating memories that last a lifetime. Our creed: Paddle hard, laugh loud, and leave no trace!

History

Founded in 1998 by a group of river enthusiasts, White Water Rafting Co. started with one raft and a passion for the river. For over 25 years, we have guided thousands of adventurers through the most thrilling rapids.
