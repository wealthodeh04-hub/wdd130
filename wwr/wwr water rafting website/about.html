OUR STORY

One raft.
Thousands of memories.

Founded in 1998 by river enthusiasts, White Water Rafting Co. began with a single raft and a passion for exploring wild waterways.

Today, our goal remains simple: help people experience the excitement of the river while treating the environment with care.

WHAT WE VALUE

Adventure

We create exciting experiences for different comfort and skill levels.

Safety

We prepare guests carefully and use appropriate equipment for every trip.

Stewardship

We respect waterways and encourage responsible outdoor recreation.
